Privacy Policy

Established: August 8, 2026

burioworks (the “Operator”) establishes the following terms regarding the handling of information in Yoin (the “App”), an app for recording live show experiences.

1. Basic Policy

Yoin is an app for saving what you felt and your memories after a live show for yourself and looking back on them.

Personal live show records that users save in Yoin, such as artist names, show information, short notes, records of what they felt, and photos, are generally stored on the user’s device.

Only when a user explicitly selects a show candidate for Shared Afterglow does the App send the minimum information needed to aggregate afterglow from the same show to the Firebase / Google Cloud infrastructure used by the Operator. The information sent and how it is handled are described in Section 4.

Yoin has no user-created account or login feature. However, Shared Afterglow uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication for authentication and duplicate-submission prevention.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following types of information on the device to provide its features.

  • Artist name, show date, show name, and venue name
  • What the user felt and short notes recorded after the show or the next day
  • Photos added by the user
  • Information about live shows the user plans to attend and information needed for notifications
  • App settings and information entered but not yet completed

Except as described in Section 3 for usage data and Section 4 for Shared Afterglow, this on-device information is not sent to the Operator.

3. Collection of Usage Data

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Firebase Analytics) to improve the App.

Sending usage data is disabled by default. Collection begins only if the user explicitly enables the sending of usage data from the Settings screen.

If enabled, the following types of information may be sent to Firebase Analytics.

  • Information about actions such as launching the App and using its features
  • App and OS versions and information about the device
  • Approximate regional information
  • An identifier used to identify the App installation

This information is used to understand how Yoin is used and to improve its features and usability. It is not used for advertising.

Yoin is designed so that the usage data it sends to Firebase Analytics does not include the contents of live show records, such as artist names, show names, venue names, short notes, records of what the user felt, or photos. Yoin also does not use the Android Advertising ID for advertising.

Users can stop sending usage data at any time from the Settings screen.

4. Information Handled for Shared Afterglow

When a user explicitly selects a show candidate, the App sends the following information so that afterglow for the “immediate” or “next day” phase can be shown collectively with afterglow from other users who selected the same show.

  • A reference to the selected shared show
  • Whether the submission is for the immediate or next-day phase
  • The afterglow position within Yoin’s in-app feeling space
  • Firebase anonymous authentication information needed for authentication and to prevent duplicate submissions to the same show and phase

The exact afterglow coordinates sent by the App are processed temporarily on the server to convert them into one cell of a 32×32 aggregate grid. The exact coordinates are not persisted in Cloud Firestore or Operator logs. The long-lived backend data is the aggregate result for the show together with the shared show information needed to display it.

To prevent duplicate submissions, the App temporarily retains a pseudonymous technical identifier scoped to one show and one phase. The raw Firebase anonymous UID is not stored as a Collective ledger in Firestore, and the system is designed not to build a cross-show history of individual users.

Your name, note, photos, and Personal MEMORY text are not sent for Shared Afterglow aggregation. Other users are not shown individual submissions or identities; they see only an aggregated distribution.

However, for shows with very few participating users, it may not be possible to completely eliminate the possibility that someone could infer another user’s general afterglow tendency by combining the aggregate with information from outside Yoin.

5. Data Export and Sharing

Users can export data saved in the App or send it outside the App using the sharing feature.

These actions are performed by the user. Information transferred to a storage destination or sharing destination selected by the user is subject to the privacy policy and other terms of that service.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Live show records stored on the device can be deleted by the user from the App. Users can also delete all Yoin data stored on the device through the Data Management feature.

Exact afterglow coordinates submitted for Shared Afterglow are not persistently stored on the server. Pseudonymous technical identifiers used for duplicate prevention and related processing are retained only for the limited period needed to close and finalize the relevant show phase, after which they are scheduled for automatic deletion. Because platform cleanup is asynchronous, physical deletion may take additional time.

Information accepted for Shared Afterglow is incorporated into an aggregate for the show. Deleting a Personal MEMORY or deleting local app data does not individually remove or withdraw information that has already been incorporated into that aggregate. The App currently does not provide an individual deletion or withdrawal function for accepted Shared Afterglow submissions. Shared show information and aggregate results may be retained to provide Shared Afterglow and future reflection experiences.

If the user stops sending usage data to Firebase Analytics, collection stops from that point onward.

For usage data already sent to Firebase Analytics, deleting data in the App does not guarantee deletion from Google’s servers. Data in Firebase Analytics is handled in accordance with the retention period set by the Operator and Google’s policies.

7. External Services and Security Measures

The App uses Firebase / Google Cloud services provided by Google LLC. Firebase Analytics is used for usage analytics, while Firebase Authentication, Cloud Functions, and Cloud Firestore are used to authenticate, process, and store information for Shared Afterglow. The App may also use security features such as Firebase App Check to help reduce unauthorized access.

Communications with these services use encrypted transport provided by Firebase / Google Cloud. Information processed by these services is handled in accordance with Google’s terms and privacy policy and the Operator’s service configuration.

Except where required by law, the Operator does not sell information obtained through the App.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be changed in response to changes to the App’s features, the external services it uses, applicable laws, platform rules, or other relevant circumstances.

Revised terms apply from the time they are posted on this page.
